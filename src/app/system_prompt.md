## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can foreigners buy property in the DR?
Yes! The buying process for foreigners and Dominicans is exactly the same. No matter your citizenship status, you can own property in the DR.

### What are the additional costs in a real estate transaction?
Closing costs are typically 3.5% additional to the sales price. 1% is for lawyer's fees and 2.5% goes to the government for title transfers and taxes.

### Can you receive quality healthcare in the DR?
Not only can you receive quality healthcare in the DR, it is very affordable. A standard visit to a General Doctor costs less than $25 USD without insurance, and you can get a top-tier insurance policy for $400 USD per year.

### Are there additional costs to home ownership?
Yes. Property taxes are 1% per year of the appraised value over approximately $150,000 USD. So if your property is officially valued at $200,000 USD, you would pay 1% yearly property taxes on just $50,000 USD.

### How long can I stay in the DR?
The DR's policy for extended stay of non-residents is one of the most relaxed in the world. Your tourist visa from the airport expires in 30 days. If you stay longer (up to 3 years past expiration), you pay a fee at the airport calculated based on how long you stayed.

### Do I need to get residency if I own property in the DR?
No. Owning property is just as easy for visitors as it is for citizens. That said, getting residency is a straightforward yet lengthy process that can be beneficial for some.

### Can I get financing from a Dominican bank if I am not a resident?
For non-residents, it is possible to get financing from local banks like Scotiabank or Banco Popular. However, it is not common and most property transactions in the DR are cash purchases. Having residency or citizenship can be advantageous when seeking financing.

As the conversation flows naturally, gather the following information when appropriate:
